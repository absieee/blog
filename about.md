## Why this exists

I wanted a place to publish small ideas without turning every thought into a polished article. 
this blog is for:
- product lessons
- technical notes
- things learned while building
- things broken while building
- useful links and reminders for future me

## How I'll use it

most posts should stay short and to the point, like how I communicate in real life. if something needs to become a proper essay, it can, but the default should be quick and useful.
